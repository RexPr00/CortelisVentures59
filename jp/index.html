プライバシーポリシー

Introduction

Cortelis Venturesは、ガバナンス、流動性計画、透明なレポーティングを備えた機関投資家向け人工知能投資を提供します。

Data collection

We collect form submissions, website analytics events, and communication records required for investor onboarding and mandate support.

Legal basis

Processing is based on consent, legitimate interest for B2B communication, and contractual necessity during due diligence phases.

Cookies

Essential cookies support language persistence and accessibility features; analytics cookies measure engagement quality.

Retention

Lead data is retained according to regulatory requirements and deleted when no longer needed for investment communication.

Third-party sharing

Data is shared only with regulated service providers, counsel, and administrators supporting execution and compliance.

Security

We apply encryption in transit, restricted access controls, and governance audits.

User rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your data where applicable.

International transfers

Cross-border transfers rely on contractual safeguards and approved legal mechanisms.

Industry risk disclosure

AI investment opportunities involve technology risk, market volatility, liquidity constraints, and regulatory change risk.
